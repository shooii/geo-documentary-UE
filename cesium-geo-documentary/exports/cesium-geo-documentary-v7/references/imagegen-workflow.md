# ImageGen workflow

Generate only after research, narration, storyboard, and the real-photo search are stable. Include region, time range, subject, use, composition, shot size, lighting, color, aspect ratio, safe area, prohibited mistakes, and artistic-reconstruction status in each prompt.

Never generate satellite imagery, administrative boundaries, official maps, statistics, or unlabelled historical evidence.

Review anatomy, architecture period, clothing, accidental text, cultural symbols, narration consistency, and crop. Store prompt, aspect ratio, status, purpose, factual status, usage, and credit label in `imagegen-manifest.json`.

Use `AI 艺术化复原`, `历史场景示意`, or `非真实影像` in the composition. Failed, timed-out, or QA-rejected images must use an explicit map placeholder.

If clean-broadcast mode prohibits visible AI wording, do not hide the label while keeping the generated image. Disable generated imagery for that delivery. Use a map-led scene or follow [boundary-and-clean-presentation.md](boundary-and-clean-presentation.md) for a verified, licensed real-photo fallback whose attribution is stored outside the frame.

Do not use ImageGen as a substitute for a real, documented living tradition such as 吴川飘色 when a precise reusable photograph is required. Generated reconstruction may illustrate an unavailable historical scene only when the delivery explicitly permits the visible reconstruction label.
